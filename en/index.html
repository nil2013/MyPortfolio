01Profile

My research focuses is on the foundational theories of law. Specifically, I examine the nature and characteristics of legal interpretation and judgment by centering on concepts such as "proof," "argument," and "argumentation," with reference to insights from the philosophy of logic and computer science. I also explore ways to apply these findings to connect legal studies with other fields, appropriately considering the differences in their respective knowledge.

Ever since encountering "Mycom BASIC Magazine" in my elementary school library, programming has been my hobby. While Scala is my favorite language, I have experience with most languages to some extent. During my student years, I worked as an engineer at three companies, gaining professional experience with Java, Scala, JavaScript, Obj-C, Swift, and Python. I am also interested in software engineering, programming language theory, and type systems, and I am exploring research themes that utilize this knowledge. I also enjoy studying positive law and am researching system development contracts.
